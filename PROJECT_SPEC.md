# Sample Custody Service

## 项目目标

Sample Custody Service is a small backend for research teams that need a reliable
chain of custody for physical specimens. It keeps the current owner, transfer
history, inspection batches, and immutable audit events in one process.

The service intentionally uses an in-memory repository so it can run locally
without a database or an online dependency. The HTTP contracts are stable enough
for a laboratory intake station and a review client.

## 用户角色

- Intake technician: registers newly received specimens and records their origin.
- Custody coordinator: transfers a specimen between people or storage locations.
- Review scientist: groups specimens into an inspection batch and closes the batch.

## 核心实体

- Specimen: the physical item, its material, quantity, lifecycle status, and current holder.
- Custody transfer: a signed handoff from one holder to another with time and note.
- Inspection batch: a set of specimens that will be reviewed together.
- Audit event: an append-only record of each state-changing action.

## 业务流程

### Register a specimen

An intake technician submits a unique code, material description, origin, quantity,
and unit. The service validates the payload, creates the specimen in `registered`
state, and emits an audit event. Duplicate codes and invalid quantities are rejected.

### Transfer custody

A custody coordinator names the current holder, the next holder, and the destination.
The service checks that the specimen is active and that the stated current holder
matches the recorded holder, then appends a transfer and updates the current holder.
Sealed specimens cannot be transferred.

### Create and complete an inspection batch

A review scientist selects active specimens and gives the group a purpose. The
service verifies that every specimen exists, is not sealed, and is not already in
another open batch. Completing a batch records the completion time and seals all
included specimens so their custody history cannot be changed afterward.

## 状态与规则

- A specimen starts as `registered`, may become `in_review`, and ends as `sealed`.
- A registered specimen can be transferred until its inspection batch completes.
- A transfer must name the current holder exactly as stored.
- A batch must contain at least one specimen and has `open` or `completed` state.
- A completed batch is immutable and all of its specimens become sealed.
- Codes, batch names, and IDs are trimmed before validation.
- All timestamps are UTC and generated by an injected clock.

## 接口与验证

The service exposes:

- `GET /healthz` for process health.
- `POST /samples` and `GET /samples` for specimen registration and listing.
- `GET /samples/{id}` for a specimen and its custody history.
- `POST /samples/{id}/transfers` for custody handoff.
- `POST /batches` and `GET /batches/{id}` for inspection batch lifecycle.
- `POST /batches/{id}/complete` to complete a batch and seal specimens.
- `GET /manifests/{id}` to return a stable text manifest for review.

Verification includes `go build ./...`, `go test ./...`, a health request, a
sample registration request, and HTTP checks for the three workflows.
